SAMPLE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
For demo/RAG testing only. This is fictional text and is not legal advice.

1. PARTIES
This Employment Agreement is between TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd. and the Employee, Mr. Sameer Fernandes.

2. POSITION
The Employee shall work as a Junior Software Developer.

3. START DATE
Employment begins on 1 August 2026.

4. COMPENSATION
The Employee shall receive a salary of INR 45,000 per month, subject to applicable taxes and deductions.

5. WORKING HOURS
The standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM from Monday to Friday.

6. PROBATION
The Employee shall be on probation for three months. During probation, either party may terminate employment by giving 7 days' written notice.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Employee shall not disclose confidential business, technical, financial, or client information of the Company during or after employment.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All software, code, documentation, designs, and inventions created by the Employee during employment and related to Company work shall belong to the Company.

9. TERMINATION
After probation, either party may terminate employment by giving 30 days' written notice or salary in lieu of notice.

10. NON-SOLICITATION
For 12 months after leaving employment, the Employee shall not directly solicit the Company's clients using confidential information gained during employment.

11. DISCLAIMER
This sample agreement is created only for software project testing and should not be used as a real legal document.
