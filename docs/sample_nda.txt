SAMPLE NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
For demo/RAG testing only. This is fictional text and is not legal advice.

1. PURPOSE
This Non-Disclosure Agreement is made to protect confidential information shared for discussion of a possible software development project.

2. DISCLOSING PARTY
The party sharing confidential information is called the Disclosing Party.

3. RECEIVING PARTY
The party receiving confidential information is called the Receiving Party.

4. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information includes business plans, source code, product designs, customer data, pricing, technical documents, passwords, and internal processes.

5. OBLIGATIONS
The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only for the stated purpose and shall not disclose it to any third party without written consent.

6. EXCLUSIONS
Information is not confidential if it is already public, already known to the Receiving Party, independently developed, or received lawfully from another source.

7. DURATION
The confidentiality obligations shall continue for 3 years from the date of disclosure.

8. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION
Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy confidential materials.

9. REMEDIES
Unauthorized disclosure may cause harm, and the Disclosing Party may seek legal remedies available under applicable law.

10. DISCLAIMER
This sample NDA is created only for software project testing and should not be used as a real legal document.
