SAMPLE PRIVACY POLICY
For demo/RAG testing only. This is fictional text and is not legal advice.

1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy explains how DemoMart collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTED
DemoMart may collect name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order history, payment status, and device information.

3. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING
User information may be used to create accounts, process orders, provide customer support, improve services, prevent fraud, and send service updates.

4. PAYMENT DATA
DemoMart does not store full card numbers. Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment service providers.

5. SHARING OF DATA
DemoMart may share limited information with delivery partners, payment processors, cloud hosting providers, and legal authorities when required by law.

6. DATA RETENTION
User data may be retained as long as necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and business records.

7. USER RIGHTS
Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting support.

8. SECURITY
DemoMart uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data. However, no system is completely secure.

9. CHILDREN
The service is not intended for users below 18 years of age.

10. CONTACT
For privacy questions, users may contact privacy@demomart.example.

11. DISCLAIMER
This sample privacy policy is created only for software project testing and should not be used as a real legal document.
