SAMPLE RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT
For demo/RAG testing only. This is fictional text and is not legal advice.

1. PARTIES
This Rental Agreement is made between the Landlord, Mr. Arun Naik, and the Tenant, Ms. Rhea D'Souza.

2. PROPERTY
The property is a residential apartment located at Flat 204, Green Valley Apartments, Margao, Goa.

3. TERM
The tenancy begins on 1 July 2026 and ends on 30 June 2027 unless renewed in writing by both parties.

4. RENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 18,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. Rent shall be paid by bank transfer.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of INR 36,000. The Landlord may deduct unpaid rent, utility charges, or repair costs caused by the Tenant.

6. USE OF PROPERTY
The property shall be used only for residential purposes. The Tenant shall not use the property for illegal activity or commercial operations.

7. MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall keep the property clean and shall inform the Landlord about major repairs. Minor day-to-day maintenance shall be the Tenant's responsibility.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice. If the Tenant leaves without notice, one month's rent may be deducted from the security deposit.

9. SUBLETTING
The Tenant shall not sublet the property or transfer possession to another person without written permission from the Landlord.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The parties shall first try to resolve disputes through discussion. If unresolved, disputes may be referred to mediation or a court having jurisdiction in Goa.

11. DISCLAIMER
This sample agreement is created only for software project testing and should not be used as a real legal document.
